## Three parts, one agent each

### 1. Topic 6: the authenticity of Sira poetry → `research/topics/shir-al-sira.md`

This is the base for grading every text of the era, so be exact.
- **Ibn Sallam on Ibn Ishaq:** his words in full, with the page.
- **Ibn Hisham's notes on the poems of his Sira:** a table of all 32. For each, give:
  - the poet and the first hemistich;
  - his words, letter for letter;
  - volume and page.
  Settle the two places the survey left open (ج2 ص176 and ص434).
- **Other ancient judgements** on Sira and Maghazi poetry, and on Futuh poetry transmitted through Sayf ibn ʿUmar.
- **Modern judgements:** Shawqi Dayf, Nasir al-Din al-Asad, and any other book in the register.
- **The rule:** how a poem of the era gets each of the four grades, given where it is transmitted (the Sira alone,
  Ibn Sallam, the diwan's recension, the Aghani) and what the ancients say of it. Give worked examples.
- **The register entry for Ibn Hisham's Sira:** the reference edition and its link, written the way `docs/sources.md`
  writes entries.

### 2. The path of Hassan ibn Thabit → `research/paths/hassan.md`

Follow the form of `research/paths/labid.md`: the criterion, the diwan's map, the path, the stories, concepts and
memorised verses, the counts, research decisions, and questions for the owner.
- **The diwan's edition.** Look for Walid ʿArafat's edition on archive.org and elsewhere. If it is not open, compare
  Hirschfeld's edition and al-Barquqi's commentary. Propose the register entry.
- **The mother poem.** The hamziyya «عفت ذات الأصابع» in Ibn Ishaq's recension, with its parts.
- **The Jahili stage of the path,** which the Jahili era will refer to: Jabala, the Ghassanids, «لله در عصابة».
- **What is ascribed to him and is not his.** Use Ibn Hisham's notes, Ibn Sallam, and «كثر عليه الحمل».

### 3. The paths of Kaʿb ibn Zuhayr and al-Hutayʾa → `research/paths/kaab-ibn-zuhayr.md` and `research/paths/al-hutayah.md`

The same form as part 2.
- **Kaʿb.**
  - Settle the recension of his diwan: al-Sukkari or Thaʿlab.
  - «بانت سعاد» is the mother poem; give its recension and edition.
  - The burda story is «منسوب»; give the evidence in full.
  - He has a link to Zuhayr's path in the Jahili era. Say where it refers to it.
- **al-Hutayʾa.**
  - The diwan, in Ibn al-Sikkit's recension and al-Sukkari's commentary: its edition and where it can be read.
  - The mother poem is the siniyya.
  - The stories: al-Zibriqan and ʿUmar's prison, and Hassan's judgement.
  - The ascriptions.
