# Brief: survey for the map of the second era, صدر الإسلام

Repo: /home/user/arabic-literature. Research only: no site content, no book, no git.

## The owner's decisions (2026-10-26)

1. **Scope.** The era runs from the Mission to the end of the Rashidun caliphate, about 40 AH. The Umayyad era is a
   separate book after it.
2. **Mukhadramun already written in the Jahili era.** al-Khansa, Labid and the others whose main material is Jahili
   stay there (`docs/jahili-map.md`, section «سادسًا: المخضرمون»; `docs/alam.md`, section 4). This era refers to them
   in one line and takes only their Islamic material: al-Khansa at al-Qadisiyya, Labid's silence, and so on. A poet
   not written in the Jahili era, such as Hassan, Kaʿb ibn Zuhayr or al-Hutayʾa, gets his full place here.

## Two parts: one agent each

### A: the figures and the faces → `research/islami/A-figures.md`

- **Formative figures (أعلام التكوين).** These are the poets the era's poetry rests on, each with a full path and a
  mother poem read in depth. Candidates include Hassan, Kaʿb ibn Zuhayr, al-Hutayʾa, Abu Dhuʾayb, Mutammim and
  al-Nabigha al-Jaʿdi. For each:
  - why he is in this tier, with sources;
  - the mother poem: its recension, its edition, and its grade;
  - the other texts, and the stories with their grades.
- **Important figures.** These get a page and several texts each, such as Kaʿb ibn Malik, ʿAbd Allah ibn Rawaha and
  al-Shammakh. Give the same evidence, briefer.
- **Faces in groups.** Propose the groups for this era, as the Jahili map has fifteen, and in each group the names as
  ▣ card or ▢ entry. Possible groups:
  - the poets of the Futuh;
  - the poets of the apostasy wars;
  - the women poets;
  - the poets of Quraysh against the Prophet who later accepted Islam;
  - the delegations;
  - the companions who composed verse;
  - the elegies.

  Test each against the sources. Place every one of the 34 names, and say where each goes.

### B: the topics, prose and sources → `research/islami/B-topics.md`

- **Topics (قضايا العصر).** Propose them with what each would cover and its sources. Test the old draft and add to it:
  - the Qurʾan and Arab taste;
  - Islam and poetry (the Qurʾan on poets, the Prophet's stance, «إن من الشعر لحكمة»);
  - poetry in the Sira and its authenticity, where Ibn Sallam blames Ibn Ishaq;
  - the poetic contest between Medina and Quraysh;
  - prose: the Farewell Sermon, the caliphs' speeches, letters and treaties, and the question of «نهج البلاغة».
- **The Qurʾan.** The site treats the Qurʾan as the text that shaped the language and literature, from literary sources,
  not tafsir. Name the sources that do this, such as al-Jahiz, al-Khattabi, al-Rummani, al-Baqillani and ʿAbd al-Qahir.
  Say which have editions on Shamela or archive.org.
- **Sources.** Check the frozen package in `docs/sources.md`. List the books this era needs that the register lacks,
  each with its reference edition and link, and why it is needed. These go to the owner for approval. Examples are the
  diwans of Hassan, Kaʿb and al-Hutayʾa, Ibn Hisham's Sira, al-Suhayli, and Ibn Saʿd.
